The ritual

Heat. Cold. Rest. Again.

Sauna is older than any of us and simpler than most things. You build a fire. You sit in the heat until you've had enough. You step out into cold air, or snow, or a lake. Then you rest, and go around again.

Somewhere in the second or third round, the noise drops away. That stillness is what we're named for.

Questions

Before you book.

How does booking and payment work?

You request dates on this site and sign the rental agreement. Your dates are held while we review the request. We then confirm the total, including delivery, and send an invoice. The booking is final once the invoice is paid.

What does my site need?

A firm, fairly level spot with clear access for a truck and trailer, kept well away from buildings, vehicles, tents and anything that can burn. In winter the path and the pad need to be cleared of snow and ice. No power or water hookup is needed.

How many people fit?

The hot room seats six to seven adults, with room for a couple more on the sides. Larger groups take turns, which is half the fun.

Is it hard to run a wood stove?

No. We walk you through lighting it, controlling the air and making steam when we deliver, and we leave written instructions. Heat-up takes about [HEAT-UP TIME].

What should we bring?

Towels, something to sit on, plenty of drinking water, clean water for the stones, and warm clothes and footwear for cooling off outside.

Can children use it?

Children under [MINIMUM AGE] may not use the hot room. Older children may, with their parent or guardian inside with them the whole time. A parent or guardian signs the agreement for each child.

Can we drink in the sauna?

No. Alcohol and heat are a dangerous mix, and the rental agreement doesn't allow alcohol or drugs before or during sauna use. Save the drink for afterwards.

Do you provide a cold plunge?

No. Many guests cool off in a lake, in snow or with their own tub. Stillfire doesn't provide, inspect or supervise any of these, and using them is at your own risk.

Why is there a waiver?

You run the sauna yourself, with a live fire, so the renter signs a rental agreement and release when booking. Every other adult signs before using it, using a link we send you.
